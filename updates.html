Updates

Short, dated notes about sites. Each one says who it comes from:

  • Primary update (solid line beside it): from an organisation on the trusted list, with a recorded consent. It names the kind of organisation, for example “From a custodian group”.
  • Community note, unverified (dashed line): anyone can send one, and nobody has checked it. A note marked Needs checking was sent as a primary update but has not passed the checks yet.
