Research library

Published sources and research about East Are’are, West Are’are and Small Malaita. Each entry links out to where the work is published; nothing is hosted here. “Cited for” shows which facts on a site profile rest on that work, so you can trace a fact back to its first source.

  • Published source (solid line beside it): a work already published elsewhere, such as a paper, report or article.
  • Submitted by the author (dashed line): a work its author offered for listing. The words “not independently reviewed” appear when nobody outside has reviewed it.

Each entry also states its kind of evidence in words. A listing is not an endorsement: the atlas has not checked any work’s findings. To submit research: . See the Field Kit for how entries are added.
